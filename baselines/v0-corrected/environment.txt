Caveat (D-g): the committed proofs are
reproducible only while those wheels remain obtainable.
If they leave PyPI, or the exact CPython 3.14.4 build and the BLAS/CPU float
behavior these wheels happened to have are gone, re-running the drivers can drift
from the committed proofs. The venv itself (/tmp/opencode/tcc-venv) is ephemeral and is not committed:
this file is a recipe, not a guarantee. The evidence of record is the committed
proofs, and the methodological note (README.md) makes no reproducibility claim
beyond them.
